# Area J: Accessibility Audit (WCAG 2.2 AA)

**Reviewer:** Senior Web Accessibility Specialist & IAAP Certified CPACC  
**Artifact:** `./audit/10-a11y.md`  
**Finding Severity:** MEDIUM (P2)

---

## 1. Automated & Static Code Inspection Summary

The public homepage (`/`) previously achieved a 100/100 Lighthouse accessibility audit following contrast remediation of Cadmium Orange (`#F96100` updated to `#BA3D00` with a 5.3:1 ratio).  
However, an audit of the deeper application routes (`/intake`, `/portal`, `/screeners/[id]`, `/telehealth/[id]`, `/admin`) reveals several significant WCAG 2.2 AA non-compliances.

---

## 2. Key Accessibility Violations

### 1. Form Label Disassociation (`htmlFor` Missing)
- **Location:** `src/app/intake/page.tsx:267-380` (17 distinct form inputs).
- **Code Pattern:**
  ```tsx
  <label className="block text-xs font-bold text-gray-700 uppercase tracking-wider mb-1">
    Full Legal Name *
  </label>
  <input type="text" value={formData.fullName} ... />
  ```
- **WCAG Success Criterion:** **1.3.1 Info and Relationships (Level A)** & **4.1.2 Name, Role, Value (Level A)**.
- **Impact on Users:** Screen reader users (NVDA, JAWS, VoiceOver) navigating by form controls hear only *"Edit text, blank"* without hearing the input's purpose ("Full Legal Name", "Date of Birth", "Emergency Contact"). Furthermore, clicking the label does not focus the input.

### 2. Sub-Minimum Font Sizes (< 12px)
- **Locations:**
  - `src/app/portal/page.tsx`: Multiple occurrences of `text-[10px]` and `text-[11px]` on clinical instructions and badge tags.
  - `src/app/telehealth/[roomId]/page.tsx`: `text-[10px]` on timer, status pills, and chat timestamps.
  - `src/components/admin/ClientChartDossier.tsx`: `text-[9px]` and `text-[10px]` on demographic and normative cutoff cards.
- **WCAG Success Criterion:** **1.4.4 Resize Text (Level AA)** & Best Practice.
- **Impact on Users:** Clients with low vision or cognitive strain struggle to decipher 9px and 10px typography, especially on mobile devices.

### 3. Missing `aria-live` Announcements on Screener Scoring
- **Location:** `src/app/screeners/[assignmentId]/page.tsx:168-200`.
- **WCAG Success Criterion:** **4.1.3 Status Messages (Level AA)**.
- **Impact on Users:** When a screener is submitted and transitions to the score summary and crisis resources, the view change is not announced to assistive technology via an `aria-live="polite"` region.

### 4. Interactive Target Size Compliance (WCAG 2.2 SC 2.5.8)
- In the screener questionnaire (`src/app/screeners/[assignmentId]/page.tsx`), the circular radio selection buttons for Likert scales have adequate padding on desktop, but on mobile screens (360px width), the touch target shrinks below the required 24x24 CSS pixels without adequate spacing.

---

## 3. Required Specification for Fix (Area J)

1. **Add `id` and `htmlFor` to all 17 Intake Form Inputs:**
   ```tsx
   <label htmlFor="intake-full-name" className="...">Full Legal Name *</label>
   <input id="intake-full-name" type="text" ... />
   ```
2. **Elevate Typography Baseline:**
   Replace all instances of `text-[9px]` and `text-[10px]` with a minimum of `text-xs` (12px) for critical clinical metadata and labels.
3. **Wrap Screener Submission in ARIA Live Region:**
   Add `role="status"` and `aria-live="polite"` to the submission confirmation container.
4. **Enforce Touch Target Sizing:**
   Ensure all Likert scale rating buttons have a minimum touch target size of 44x44px on mobile devices.
