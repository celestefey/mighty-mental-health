## 2. Trauma-Informed UI Design Deficits

1. **No "Quick Exit" Safety Button:**  
   In mental health platforms dealing with family violence, trauma, and domestic abuse, a persistent *"Quick Exit"* button (which immediately redirects to a neutral site like Google or weather.com.au and clears page state) is an essential trauma-informed safeguard.
2. **Cannot Save In-Progress Intake:**  
   The 4-step intake requires entering medical history, psychiatric history, and trauma goals. For clients with severe anxiety, PTSD, or ADHD, filling out extensive forms in a single sitting can trigger overwhelm and drop-off.
3. **Demo UI Leaking into Production:**  
   The interface is cluttered with prototype artifacts:
   - *"Clinician Fast-Unlock (Demo Access)"*
   - *"Seed Test Cohort"*
   - *"Switch Client Persona (Sarah Jenkins / Marcus Vance)"*  
   These buttons erode client trust and create severe data integrity risks if left visible to real users.

## 3. The Build-vs-Buy Verdict for a Solo Practitioner

### The Strategic Dilemma:
Luke Piatti is a solo-practitioner counsellor in Logan, QLD. Building a 100% custom EHR, video conferencing system, booking engine, Medicare-compliant invoicing system, and RLS database from scratch carries **extreme maintenance overhead, security liability, and regulatory compliance risk**.

| Component | Recommendation | Strategic Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Public Website & Branding** (`/`, `/contact`) | **KEEP CUSTOM (Next.js)** | Exceptional brand identity, high conversion potential, authentic positioning of Luke's exercise-integrated modality. |
| **Psychometric Screener Engine** (`/screeners`) | **KEEP CUSTOM / HYBRID** | Highly differentiated; immediate scoring of DASS-21, somatic maps, and trajectory deltas is a major clinical asset. |
| **Client Intake Portal** (`/intake`) | **KEEP CUSTOM** | Beautiful 4-step onboarding capturing Luke's unique somatic movement profile. |
| **Telehealth Video** (`/telehealth`) | **REPLACE / EMBED** | **Do not build a custom WebRTC stack.** Embed **Daily.co** (Sydney region), **Coviu**, or integrate **Cal.com Video / Whereby**. |
| **EHR Notes & GP Letters** (`/admin`) | **HYBRID OR INTEGRATE** | Maintain as a specialized clinical scribe for exercise-integrated therapy, OR integrate with an established Australian allied-health EHR like **Halaxy** or **Cliniko** via API. |
| **Billing & Payments** | **REPLACE** | Integrate **Stripe Checkout** or use Halaxy/Cliniko native automated invoicing and Medicare/Health Fund claiming. |
