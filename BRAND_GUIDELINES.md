### 1.2 The "Dual-Track" Horizon: The Somatic-Cognitive Synthesis
Traditional healthcare treats the mind and body in isolated silos:
* **Conventional Therapy** frequently ignores the neurobiological state of the body—elevated cortisol, nervous system dysregulation, physical tension, sedentary inertia, and metabolic distress.
* **Conventional Fitness** frequently ignores psychological trauma, shame, perfectionism, chronic burnout, and underlying emotional suffering.

**Mighty Mental Health exists at the intersection.**  
We recognize that the nervous system is embodied. Moving the body shifts neurochemistry, regulation, and somatic self-worth; exploring the psyche illuminates motivations, unpacks trauma, and dissolves the emotional barriers preventing sustained physical vitality. 

```
   PSYCHOTHERAPY (Mind)                       PHYSICAL FITNESS (Body)
   * Root cause inquiry                        * Somatic regulation & stress release
   * Narrative reframing                       * Neurochemical balance (endorphins, BDNF)
   * Trauma resolution                         * Physical strength, posture, stamina
   * Personal values & meaning                 * Nervous system regulation (vagal tone)
               \                                    /
                \                                  /
                 v                                v
          +----------------------------------------------+
          |         MIGHTY MENTAL HEALTH (MMH)           |
          |       Concurrent Somatic & Mind Care         |
          +----------------------------------------------+
```

### 1.3 The 3 Uniques (Core Differentiators)

1. **Fitness + Mental Health Under One Registered Clinician**  
   Clients do not have to negotiate conflicting advice between a disconnected personal trainer and a detached psychologist. One trusted practitioner guides both dimensions with dual clinical qualification.
2. **Accessible, Down-to-Earth Care Without Clinical Sterility**  
   No intimidating clinical jargon, diagnostic judgment, or cold waiting rooms. Meeting clients "at their level" with authenticity, practical tools, and unpretentious respect.
3. **From Symptom Relief to Tangible Human Strength**  
   We do not stop at "feeling less bad." We actively build physical stamina, mental agency, self-determination, and a life of vibrant, purposeful action.

### 2.2 Deep-Dive Value Frameworks & Clinical Application

#### Pillar 1: Human Dignity
* **The Definition**: *Respecting your inherent worth as a human being.*
* **Clinical Rationale**: Every person who walks through our physical or virtual doors carries inviolable value, regardless of their struggle, diagnosis, socioeconomic status, or past setbacks.
* **Client Experience**: You are never treated as a pathology or a ticket number; you are treated as a complete human with innate dignity.
* **How We Live This Value**: We maintain absolute confidentiality, transparent pricing, unconditional positive regard, and gentle, respectful communication.

#### Pillar 2: Acceptance
* **The Definition**: *Leaving judgements at the door to focus on what's really going on for you.*
* **Clinical Rationale**: Shame is the greatest inhibitor of psychological healing. True transformation begins only when an individual feels safe enough to tell the unvarnished truth without fear of condemnation.
* **Client Experience**: No shame about missed workouts, emotional relapses, relationship mistakes, or past coping mechanisms.
* **How We Live This Value**: We actively normalize human vulnerability. We unpack behaviors as protective adaptations rather than character defects.

#### Pillar 3: Person-Centred Care
* **The Definition**: *Meeting you where you are in your journey — getting onto the same level.*
* **Clinical Rationale**: The therapeutic relationship is egalitarian. The client is the expert in their own lived experience; the clinician is a collaborative guide.
* **Client Experience**: We don't preach from an ivory tower. We listen first, understand your unique context, and tailor the treatment plan to your reality.
* **How We Live This Value**: Collaborative goal-setting; flexible pacing; meeting client communication preferences (video vs phone); adapting physical fitness to current bodily capacity without guilt.

#### Pillar 4: Self-Determination
* **The Definition**: *Empowering you with the practical tools to take control, make decisions, and grow.*
* **Clinical Rationale**: Therapy must build independence, not perpetual reliance on the therapist. Autonomy is the cornerstone of psychological resilience.
* **Client Experience**: You leave sessions with actionable strategies, somatic tools, movement protocols, and cognitive frameworks you can own for life.
* **How We Live This Value**: Transparent treatment roadmaps; skill-building exercises; encouraging client choice at every crossroad.

#### Pillar 5: Holistic Perspective
* **The Definition**: *Caring for you as a whole person — integrating brain, body, spirit, and social world.*
* **Clinical Rationale**: Depression and anxiety express themselves through muscular rigidity, gut distress, sleep deprivation, and inflammation. Fitness informs mood; mental clarity fuels physical consistency.
* **Client Experience**: We inquire about your sleep, nutrition, physical activity, and social connections just as carefully as your thought patterns and emotional history.
* **How We Live This Value**: Dual-track interventions pairing cognitive-affective counselling with somatic regulation and progressive physical fitness.

```
   |         * (Alt: White Cutout Heart)      * Authoritative, grounded, human     |

```

* **The Icon (The Somatic Lifter)**:  
  An unpretentious human figure raising a barbell overhead with an energetic, grounded stance. In the center of the chest beats an orange heart (or white cutout).  
  *Symbolism*: Physical strength driven by emotional vulnerability; lifting the heavy weights of life with heart, endurance, and courage.
* **The Official Heart Variations**:
  1. **Primary Active Colorway (Cadmium Orange Heart `#F96100`)**:  
     The standard, full-color identity mark used on digital websites, social media, presentation decks, and full-color print collateral. The orange heart conveys living metabolic warmth and emotional vitality.
  2. **Monochrome / Cutout Colorway (White Heart Cutout)**:  
     Single-color version where the heart is rendered as a clean negative-space cutout. Required for single-color printing, black-and-white forms, stamps, laser engraving, and embroidered apparel.
* **The Monogram (`mh` / `mmh`)**:  
  Features an anatomical brain convolution within the arches of `m`, and the lifter figure forming the stem of `h`. Used on favicons, social avatars, and app icons.

### 4.3 Logo Misuse (Strict Rules)
1. **DO NOT** change the color of the heart from the official Cadmium Orange (`#F96100`).
2. **DO NOT** skew, stretch, or alter the aspect ratio of the lifter figure or text.
3. **DO NOT** place the Navy logo over dark background images without using the white inverted mark (`logo-mark-inverted.svg`).
4. **DO NOT** substitute the display wordmark with generic cursive or script fonts.
5. **DO NOT** apply drop shadows, 3D extrusions, or outer glows to the vector marks.

#### Dual-Column Booking Architecture & Interaction Specifications
1. **Side-by-Side Synchronisation**:
   * Placing the session selector directly adjacent to the weekly availability grid eliminates page bouncing and multi-step friction.
   * Selecting **40-Min Initial Consult**, **60-Min Psychotherapy**, or **30-Min Somatic Consult** dynamically filters open slots (accounting for a mandatory 10-minute clinical documentation and rest buffer between appointments).
2. **Modality Switcher**:
   * Prominent toggle between **Video Call** (secure encrypted telehealth link generated automatically) and **Phone Consultation**.
3. **Time Zone Intelligence**:
   * Fixed base in **AEST (Brisbane / Logan, QLD)** with automatic browser-detected conversion for southern states on AEDT (+1 hr) and Western Australia on AWST (-2 hrs).
4. **Mobile Responsiveness**:
   * On mobile viewports (< 880px), the component seamlessly stacks into an intuitive 2-step accordion (Step 1: Choose Service Card -> Step 2: Open Slot Picker appears immediately beneath).
5. **No Upfront Financial Friction**:
   * Emphasises transparent clinical care: zero upfront card charges to reserve an initial session, reducing hesitation for vulnerable individuals seeking support.
